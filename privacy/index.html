How your information is handled

Version: 13 September 2026. This notice covers information submitted through dannymaddock.com and the site’s privacy-focused analytics.

Who is responsible

Danny Maddock, trading as BLK BX, is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to danny@blkbx.uk.

Information the enquiry form collects

The form asks for your name, email address, enquiry type and message. You may also provide a company name, current tools, team size, budget range and preferred start timeframe. The system records the privacy-notice version, page path, optional campaign parameters, a submission identifier, submission time and review status.

Please do not submit passwords, access credentials, special-category information or other sensitive personal information. The spam honeypot is not stored. The contact process does not intentionally store raw IP addresses or a full browser fingerprint.

Why the information is used

The information is used to review and respond to your enquiry, understand the requested work, manage follow-up, prevent duplicate submissions and protect the service from misuse. The usual lawful basis is legitimate interests in operating the consultancy and responding to people who ask about its services. Where another basis is required, it will be applied as appropriate.

Storage, access and notifications

Submissions are stored in a privately operated database. They are not made publicly readable. Access is restricted to the server-side contact process and authorised administrative review. A minimal notification may be sent to an internal mailbox; it contains the submission identifier, name, optional company, enquiry type and time, but not the full message or sender email address.

Retention and manual review

Each submission is reviewed manually. Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage legitimate follow-up and meet applicable legal or accounting requirements. Records are periodically reviewed and deleted or anonymised when they are no longer needed. A more specific retention schedule may apply if an enquiry becomes a client engagement.

Analytics

When enabled, the site uses Umami to understand aggregate visits, successful form submissions and selected engagement such as direct-contact or professional-profile clicks. Form contents are not sent to analytics. Analytics is not used for advertising profiles or speculative personal-data collection.

Service providers and transfers

Limited information may be processed by infrastructure and email-delivery providers solely to operate the website, store enquiries and deliver the minimal internal notification. Appropriate safeguards should be reviewed before any provider or public route is enabled.

Your choices and rights

You may ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain processing. These rights can depend on the circumstances and applicable law. Contact the address above to make a request.

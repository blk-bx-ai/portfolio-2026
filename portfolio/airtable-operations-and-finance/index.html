// 01 walkthrough
Operations /
Student Pipeline

Enquiry to exit, in one pipeline

Enquiries, onboarding, active students, unsuccessful referrals and exited students share one lifecycle view, with a tab per campus. Open any card and the student’s record carries everything linked to them. The sidebar holds four role-based interfaces over the same base.

Operations /
Campus Timetable

The session at the centre

Every booking is a session record linking student, staff, attendance and staffing. Student sessions and staff coverage sit on one page — scroll inside the window to see both, so a gap in cover shows up next to the session it affects.
